Measured acquisition context
Instrument measurements belong to the acquisition record. They are not measurements of the selected terminal or a physiological validation of its neighbours.


Observation and model assumptions
Skeleton paths are visible image evidence, not anatomical midribs or whole-leaf segmentation. Missing terminals may result from occlusion, truncation, rejected masks or extraction failure. A newly observed path does not establish leaf birth; an unobserved path does not establish loss.
Association uses partial one-to-one assignment under retained rotation-phase alternatives. Assignment margins and phase support are numerical sensitivity summaries, not calibrated identity probabilities. Camera blending is a training-selected image-alignment surrogate and does not authenticate tissue correspondence.


All images, masks, graph evidence and measured context used by this page are embedded. No external services are requested. Source hashes and fixed missing-frame slots remain available in the selection export.
