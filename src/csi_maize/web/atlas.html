MATERIALS AND METHODS

        
The overlap and calculation are part of the method

        

          We paired acquisition and filename index, retained the frozen affine
          transform, and sampled NoIR into RGB coordinates. RGB-only maps use
          eroded RGB plant support. NoIR-only maps use sampled eroded NoIR
          support. Cross-camera maps use the union of accepted candidate leaf
          common interiors. Whole-plant silhouette agreement is displayed
          separately.
        

        

          We guarded dark and clipped processing-grid channels before affine
          interpolation and required all contributing NoIR samples to pass. We
          calculated nonlinear NoIR indices after sampling the channels; this
          differs from warping a native-camera ratio. Each formula retains its
          own validity mask. We did not select registration transforms using the
          resulting index values.
        

        

          We retain core registration, signal exclusions, support definitions
          and replication policy in the main paper. The complete formula table,
          all-acquisition coverage and exhaustive atlases accompany the
          supplementary methods and data repository.

Formula catalogue and interpretation

        

          R,G,B denote recorded RGB DN; NoIR channels retain their camera names.
          Σ denotes the sum within a camera and GM its geometric mean. The six
          multichannel formulas are exploratory descriptors, not established
          physiological indices. The15pairwise differences are redundant: at a
          positive six-channel pixel they contain at most five independent
          relative coordinates.
