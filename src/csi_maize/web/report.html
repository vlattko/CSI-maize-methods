03 / SIGNED CHANGES, SHARED DOMAIN

Move a neighbour through
the prescribed angles

Rigid donor rotations retain the receiver geometry and isolated footprint. A common raster domain encloses all configured poses. Receiver gain can differ from whole-shoot change because projected area is redistributed among organs and escape.

The table retains every prescribed baseline angle on the finest available grid. Embedded numerical data also retain all supplied configurations and resolutions. These are model rotations without collision, contact or elastic mechanics.

__REPOSITION__
04 / WHAT DOES THE INVERSE FIT RECOVER?

Check geometry and downstream effects separately

Known-answer diagnostics supply privileged identity-labelled 2D midrib paths, including hidden portions, and known cameras and angles. They test a declared assisted fitting configuration. They do not validate raw-image organ discovery or independent real-plant anatomy.

Surface distance is a symmetric nearest-vertex mean at the declared sampling grid, in arbitrary synthetic units. Withheld masks are clean synthetic views. A better mask or sampled-geometry score need not improve the directed-release endpoint. Every recorded fit and failure is retained.

__RECOVERY__
05 / SUPPORTING OBSERVATION LAYER

Two cameras.
Inspect the overlap before the index.

The spatial atlas supports observation inspection: registered RGB and longpass NoIR projections, their disagreement, and guarded camera-DN descriptors. Nominal filename pairing and affine agreement do not establish synchronized exposure or corresponding tissue.

NoIR channels are processed overlapping camera responses. These maps are not calibrated reflectance, independently measured NIR bands or chlorophyll estimates. Angular views repeat an acquisition; they are not independent biological replicates.

__INTERPRETATION__

Equal beam weights are designed scenarios, not daily illumination. The accepted finite geometry family is a sensitivity analysis, not a biological posterior. Unmatched organs and unobserved tissue remain unresolved rather than being assigned emergence, death or persistent identity.
