# Full cohort methods and evidence

The full-cohort extension separates observation, anatomical identity, geometry and the endpoint of an architectural experiment. These components require different validation. Source-assisted surfaces support inspectable fixed-receiver interventions. Automatic foregrounds and terminal paths remain hypotheses until tested against independent organ references.

## Executed population

The native JPEG decode audit retained 362 acquisition directories. Of 28,312 image paths, 27,286 decoded fully; 1,022 were empty and four nonempty streams were damaged. Two complete acquisitions lacked identified reference keys. The analysis therefore extracted 27,126 identified frames and kept all 360 keyed assay records. Source completeness gave 334 acquisitions with 40 decodable pairs.

Foreground quality rules accepted 26,310 frames. Primary summaries require at least 20 accepted views across all four quadrants: 339 RGB, 329 NoIR and 329 paired records. The 39 base traits yield 117 median/maximum/IQR features per camera. Missing images and measurements remain missing. The original core ingest was separately executed against the full raw archive and returned 362 directories and 334 eligible acquisitions; its 39 generated outputs passed stored-output verification.

Full rotational analysis retained 5,344 harmonic fits and 646,400 phase/budget rows from the selected subset of 505 camera acquisitions with all 40 masks accepted. Of 144 repeated-label camera trajectories, 105 met primary coverage at every visit. Automatic RGB terminal fitting returned 377 hypotheses; 2,495 of 3,721 held-out track/view rows met the positional gate, with terminals available in 3,523. These are compatibility counts, not annotated leaf accuracy.

## Known geometry and endpoint checks

Fourteen original Pheno4D scans represented seven maize plants at two labelled dates. Under known render cameras and supplied foreground, terminal fitting recovered 40 distinct instance-date regions among 67 observable regions, with 72 regions in total. Automatic temporal assignment predicted 16 correct links among 16 predictions, recovering 16 of 36 true common regions overall. Supplied-instance centroid matching already recovered 36/36 tip-convention common regions, so the richer descriptor did not show an advantage over that simple comparator. A different-plant control accepted many physically false geometric links.

Known geometry supported 15,520 fixed-receiver removal evaluations. We retained both annotation conventions, the separate collar stem, complete receiver denominators and all directional sensitivity results. Discretization and point-footprint sensitivity reached 0.271 receiver-footprint fractions. Projected exposure is not absorbed radiation or photosynthesis.

The 15-case CSI geometry diagnostic retained camera alternatives and an exploratory training-supported domain repeat. Median case-level withheld silhouette IoU was 0.392 after training-only camera selection. This addresses nominal clipping without validating anatomy. DA3 evaluated five declared camera hypotheses on a separate fixed three-view protocol: 145 configurations executed, five skipped training-mask checks, and none failed during inference. Aligned learned-pose projections had zero median withheld IoU in both camera modalities. No camera winner was selected using the withheld view.

## Supporting observations and prediction

Paired-camera analysis retained all 1,440 quarter-view anchors, 12,230 registration/control rows and 330,210 per-formula rows. Nominal median IoU rose from 0.598 with moment initialization to 0.845 with ECC. Dark NoIR green output made many six-channel formulas unavailable. Each formula retained its own support; only two acquisitions had all 27 formulas. The focused DN baseline uses the same guarded common-overlap support as its cross-channel formulas.

Prediction comprised 84 completed experiments and 270,132 independently checked row-model records. Preprocessing and ridge tuning use training folds. Held-out genotype, held-out treatment and shuffled-trajectory controls remain distinct. SAM traits and frozen DINO descriptors did not improve fluorescence prediction over metadata here. A small genotype-split NIR descriptor improvement did not persist under treatment transfer. These are within-experiment associations rather than additional spectral measurements or external physiological validation.

## Scope of software validation

The source contains known-answer tests, synthetic degeneracies, mask/source binding checks, strict keyed joins, output audits and false-correspondence controls. The newly assembled coordinator has additional ownership, locking, input/output integrity and dependency tests. Its plan and producer argument contracts are checked separately from the executed scientific stages. The validation record does not claim that the new coordinator reran all images in a second independent workspace.

Physical pot continuity, the exact exposure schedule, destructive-assay correspondence and camera calibration remain author/protocol questions. The methods use recorded visits and repeated-label candidates until those facts are independently established. Data and reference outputs will be made available through Zenodo after publication.
